Why clinics are moving off spreadsheets
and paper charts

Most of the friction in day-to-day care doesn't come from medicine — it comes from information sitting in the wrong place at the wrong time.

Risk found late

Conditions like diabetes or hypertension are often flagged only after symptoms already show.

Scans wait on availability

X-rays and imaging depend entirely on a specialist being free to review them.

Records live in silos

Reports, prescriptions and notes end up scattered across separate systems.

No one to ask

Patients and doctors alike have no quick way to get a straight answer about a report.
